Important Billing Information & Bulk Billing Eligibility: The majority of our standard weekday consultations are BULK BILLED from Monday to Saturday for eligible patients:
  • Children 15 years of age and under
  • Patients with a current Health Care Card
  • Pensioner Concession Card holders
  • Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Gold Card holders
  • Seniors and Patients 65 years of age and over
Please note: All patients on Sundays and Public Holidays are fully privately billed. Selected procedures (Iron Infusions, Skin Lesion Excision, Ear Syringing) require private fees.
